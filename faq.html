Video editing agency FAQ

Straight answers about how EditorsUnited works — services, pricing, turnaround, revisions, and languages. We are based in Montréal and serve clients across Canada and the USA.

What services does EditorsUnited offer?

EditorsUnited is a video editing agency specializing in short-form video: TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. We also edit talking-head videos, produce voiceovers, and create bilingual English/French versions of the same video. Every edit includes hook optimization, captions, and CTA framing.

Who is EditorsUnited for?

Our clients are coaches, financial advisors, real-estate agents, and content creators who want their content to generate inquiries and booked calls — not just views. We work with businesses and creators across Canada and the USA.

How much does it cost, and what is included?

We offer three monthly packages, priced in CAD: Starter at $799/month (about 7 videos per month), Growth at $1,499/month (15 videos per month, our most popular plan), and Scale at $2,699/month (30 videos per month). Annual billing is about 15% less. Every plan includes hook optimization, caption work, and CTA framing; Growth and Scale add a dedicated editor and strategy calls.

How fast will I get my videos?

Turnaround is typically 2-3 business days per video, depending on your plan: Starter edits are delivered within 5 business days, Growth within 3 business days, and Scale within 48 hours.

What is your revision policy?

Starter includes 1 revision round per video, Growth includes 2 revision rounds, and Scale includes unlimited revisions. The full details are on our policies page.

Do you work in French, English, or both?

Both. EditorsUnited is a bilingual Montréal team and delivers videos in English, French, or both — including English and French versions of the same video.

Where are you located? Do you work remotely?

We are based in Montréal, Québec, Canada, and work fully remotely with clients across Canada and the United States. Footage upload, review rounds, and calls all happen online.

How does onboarding work?

You book a call through Calendly, pick a package and subscribe, then upload your raw footage to the client portal. We start with a strategy call to lock in your voice, hooks, and CTAs before the first edit.

Do you edit long-form videos?

Our focus is short-form, but we also edit talking-head videos such as YouTube videos, webinars, and podcast clips. If you have a long-form project, contact us and we will confirm scope and pricing.

How do I get started?

Book a free call on our contact page (scheduling runs on Calendly), or email support@editorsunited.com. If you already know your package, you can subscribe directly from the pricing section on the home page.

Still have questions?
Book a free 15-minute call or email us at support@editorsunited.com — we reply within 24 hours.
